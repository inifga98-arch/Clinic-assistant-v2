# Flow E (prepare a session) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** 📋 Preparar sesión sends a short, fact-grounded brief for a patient, then offers the next step.

**Architecture:** pure fact-building and formatting (`prep-logic.ts`, `prep-format.ts`), one AI call
for the last-session line and suggestions (`lib/ai/prep-brief.ts`) guarded by code, data loading
(`prep-store.ts`) and a small step machine (`prep.ts`) wired into `index.ts`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Supabase, OpenAI Structured Outputs (zod).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-flow-e-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- es-MX text; one message for the brief, under ~120 words for a normal patient; ≤ 1,600 chars.
- Confirmed sessions only. Flow writes nothing.
- Every number in the brief comes from code or survives the number guard.
- Simulate as Terapeuta Simulador; seed logs only for test patients.

## Review Focus

- Patient with no confirmed session and no plan: brief still useful, no crash, no AI invention.
- Last session today: window of 0 days, says so.
- AI call throws or times out: brief still sent without the AI lines.
- AI cites a fact that doesn't exist or invents a number: that line is dropped.
- A voice note or a name of another therapist's patient at the picker.
